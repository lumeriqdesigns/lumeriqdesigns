What website services do you offer?

Lumeriq Designs builds business websites, service websites, landing pages, portfolio sites and custom web interfaces with responsive design and search-friendly foundations.

Do you provide local SEO?

Yes. Local SEO work can include on-page optimization, technical improvements, local search signals, internal linking and support for businesses that want to improve their visibility in relevant local searches.

Yes. Google Business Profile services include setup and optimization support such as business information, categories, services, photos, profile completeness and local presence.
