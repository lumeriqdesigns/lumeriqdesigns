Search Visibility

Tracked in Google Search Console: total clicks grew to 12.4K (+45.6%) and impressions to 389K (+38.7%) over a 6-month window, with top queries like "seo services near me" and "local seo company" driving the bulk of the traffic. A parallel SEMrush audit showed an Authority Score of 46 and organic traffic climbing alongside a growing backlink profile.

Google Business Profile

Full Google Business Profile setup and optimization to strengthen local visibility, tracking business views, search appearances, calls and customer actions directly from the Business Profile dashboard.

Website Performance (PageSpeed)

Technical and on-page fixes turned a slow, underperforming site into one that scores in the high 90s on Google PageSpeed Insights, covering performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO, with matching gains on Core Web Vitals across both mobile and desktop.
