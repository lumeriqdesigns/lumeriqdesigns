Portfolio / Aduni Wears

Aduni Wears

A fashion e-commerce site built for a modern, story-driven shopping experience across every screen size.

ClientAduni Wears
ServiceWeb Development
FocusE-commerce / Fashion
Year2026

Overview

Aduni Wears needed a website that felt as premium as the clothing it sells: a place where new-season collections and a clear brand story could sit alongside a smooth, modern shopping experience.

Challenge

Fashion e-commerce lives or dies on first impressions and how well it works on mobile, since that's where most shoppers browse and buy. The site needed strong visual storytelling, an easy path from browsing to checkout, and layouts that hold up cleanly on phones, tablets and desktops alike.

Solution

Designed and built a fully responsive storefront with a bold hero section, live order/cart previews, a "Shop Collections" grid and a newsletter signup, all tested and refined across mobile, tablet and desktop breakpoints so the experience feels intentional on every device.

Gallery

Technology

HTML / CSS / JS Responsive Design Mobile-First Layout E-commerce UI/UX SEO-Ready Structure

Result

A clean, on-brand storefront that presents new collections with confidence and gives shoppers a consistent, frictionless experience whether they land on mobile, tablet or desktop.
