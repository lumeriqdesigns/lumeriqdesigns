About

Web developer & SEO expert with purpose.

I'm Olawale Owolabi, the founder of Lumeriq Designs. I'm a results-driven web developer and SEO expert with over 2 years of experience helping businesses establish a powerful online presence. I build websites and handle the SEO and Google Business Profile side too, so clients don't have to coordinate between three different freelancers to get one thing done properly.

I specialize in building fast, responsive and user-friendly websites that convert visitors into customers. As an SEO and Google Business Profile expert, I help brands rank higher on search engines, attract the right audience, and grow their visibility locally and globally. My mission is to deliver modern, high-performing digital solutions that drive real growth and long-term success.
